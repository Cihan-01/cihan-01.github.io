# Basr Blocker privacy policy

_Last updated: 13 October 2026_

**Basr Blocker collects no data.** It has no servers, no accounts, no analytics and no advertising, and it makes no network requests of its own.

## What stays on your device

Basr Blocker saves your settings in your browser's own extension storage on your device:

- the sites, pages and keywords you block, and your exceptions,
- which ready-made lists are switched on,
- your settings lock: the time it ends and, if you set a PIN, a one-way hash of it (never the PIN itself).

This information never leaves your device unless you export a backup file yourself. Uninstalling Basr Blocker deletes it.

## Pages you visit

To block things, Basr Blocker looks at web addresses and, on some pages, at page content, **on your device only**:

- **Web addresses** are checked against your blocks and keywords by the browser's built-in blocking engine.
- **Search results:** on search engines, it reads which website each image result or site icon comes from, to hide those from blocked sites.
- **Reddit, X, Tumblr and Mastodon:** while the adult list is on, it checks whether those sites have marked a post as NSFW, to hide it. On X this includes reading the "possibly sensitive" flag in the data X sends to your browser. Mastodon servers can be on any domain, so each page is checked for the Mastodon web app first.
- **SafeSearch** (only if you switch on "Force SafeSearch"): searches on Google, Bing, DuckDuckGo, Brave, Yahoo and Yandex get the search engine's own strict-filter setting added to their web address.
- **Page text** (only if you switch on "Also check page text"): it checks the page's title and text for your keywords.

None of this is recorded, stored or sent anywhere. Basr Blocker has no browsing history and no record of what it blocked.

## When you remove it

After Basr Blocker is removed, your browser opens a short goodbye page at cihan-01.github.io. The page address contains nothing about you or your settings. GitHub, which hosts the page, sees an ordinary page visit, as with any website.

## Third parties

Basr Blocker shares nothing with anyone. The ready-made lists are bundled inside the extension and are never downloaded at run time.

## Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be published here with a new date. Basr Blocker will not start collecting data without a new version of the extension and a clear notice.

# Basr Blocker

A practical website, image and keyword blocker for Firefox, Chrome, Edge, Opera and Brave. Ready-made lists, your own blocks and exceptions, keyword blocking, optional SafeSearch, and a settings lock. Everything runs on your device; nothing is sent anywhere.
